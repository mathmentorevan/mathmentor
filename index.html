About the initiative

Meet Evan, Your MathMentor.

Hi, I'm Evan! I'm a Year 12 student who has studied in both the United States and Australia. Mathematics has been one of my biggest passions for as long as I can remember.

I'm an abacus graduate and a multiple-time winner of abacus competitions. Learning abacus sharpened my mental maths, concentration and love of numbers — and inspired me to share that excitement with others.

I particularly enjoy helping younger children discover that maths can be fun, and I offer patient, encouraging support for students up to Year 10. My approach focuses on building understanding, confidence and strong problem-solving skills rather than memorising answers.
