Meet your tutor

Hi, I'm Evan!

I'm a Year 12 student educated in both the United States and Australia, with a passion for maths and helping younger students enjoy learning.

I'm an abacus graduate and have won multiple abacus competitions. I love showing children how maths works, explaining ideas patiently and helping students through Year 10 build their skills and confidence.

My approach is simple: understand the concept, work through examples, practise, and learn from mistakes — without pressure to just memorise answers.
