How lessons work

Understand. Practise. Grow.

Every learner is different. Evan aims to make maths approachable, explain the thinking behind each method and encourage students to ask questions.

1

Understand the starting point

Talk through goals, identify tricky concepts and find the right level to begin.

2

Learn the why

Use clear explanations, worked examples and patient guidance instead of memorising methods without understanding.

3

Practise for confidence

Apply the concepts, discuss mistakes constructively and revisit topics when needed.

About your tutor

Meet Evan

Hi, I'm Evan! I'm a Year 12 student who has been educated in both the United States and Australia. I've always had a strong passion for mathematics and enjoy helping others discover how satisfying it can be when a challenging idea finally clicks.

I'm an abacus graduate and have won multiple abacus competitions. My abacus journey helped strengthen my mental arithmetic, concentration and enthusiasm for numbers.

What I enjoy most is working with younger children and supporting students through Year 10. I aim to create a relaxed, encouraging environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, developing problem-solving skills and learning at their own pace.

At MathMentor with Evan, my goal is to make maths make sense — not just to find the correct answer, but to understand how to get there.
